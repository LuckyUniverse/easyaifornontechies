Questions

What is easyai?

easyai teaches non-technical people how to use AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude Code, Copilot and Perplexity. Instead of watching videos, you paste a short lesson into the tool itself and it walks you through a real task, one step at a time. The lessons are free and work on the free plans.

How does a lesson actually work?

You copy a few lines of text and paste them into ChatGPT. That turns it into a tutor for that one lesson: it gives you a step, waits while you do it, asks what happened, and adapts to your answer. Nothing to install, and nothing to log into except the tool itself.

What if it starts running ahead of me?

Every lesson ships with one line you can paste to put it back: “Go back to being the tutor. One step at a time. I was on step 3.” We test each lesson for exactly that before it goes out.

Do I have to pay for an AI tool to follow along?

No. Everything in the starter kit works on the free versions. If a paid plan is ever genuinely worth it, we'll say so plainly and explain exactly what it buys you.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything happens in your web browser — the same place you check email. No downloads, no setup, nothing to break.

What if I'm really, really not a computer person?

Then you're exactly who this was built for. If you can type a text message, you can do this. We assume nothing and skip no steps.

Won't this be out of date in six months?

The buttons move; the skills don't. We teach how to think about working with AI, so when the tools change — and they will — you adapt in an afternoon instead of starting over.

Am I too late to start?

No. Most people are further behind than they admit, and the gap between "never tried it" and "genuinely useful" is about a fortnight of ten-minute lessons.

Will my email get sold or spammed?

No. One lesson a week, and the occasional note when something genuinely matters. One click to leave, and we won't chase you.
